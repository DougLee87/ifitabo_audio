Privacy Policy for Ifitabo

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Ifitabo ("the app," "we," "us") is an English and Icibemba literature app for Android. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect directly

Ifitabo does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other identifying information ourselves. The following is stored only on your own device, and is never sent to us:

  • Your reading preferences — font style, font size, dark mode setting, and language selection.
  • Your reading progress — the last chapter and scroll position you reached in each book, and any chapters you've marked as favorites.
  • Audio files you choose to download for offline listening, and any edits you make to chapter text.

This on-device data stays on your device until you clear it yourself (for example, via Settings → Clear Downloaded Audio) or uninstall the app.

Advertising

Ifitabo shows banner ads served through Google AdMob. To display and measure these ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process technical information such as your device's advertising identifier, general (IP-based) location, app usage, and other data used for ad delivery and fraud prevention. This processing is carried out by Google, not by us directly — see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps for details.

Where required (including for users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent to ad personalization and data processing before any ad is requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can review or change that choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Options within the app. You can also manage ad personalization for your Google account generally at myadcenter.google.com, or opt out of interest-based ads at the device level in your Android settings.

Other services the app uses

  • Google Play services — the app uses the Play In-App Review API to let you rate the app without leaving it; this is handled entirely by Google Play and subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • Ifitabo does not use any analytics, crash reporting, or tracking service of our own beyond what's described above.

Children's privacy

Ifitabo is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we'll address it.

Data security

Since the app doesn't collect or transmit personal information to us, there's no account or personal data on our end to secure. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own operating system and app-sandboxing protections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the app's features change. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
